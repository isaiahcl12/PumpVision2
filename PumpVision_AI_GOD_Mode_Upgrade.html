Defaults show every new token, best score first. High-risk tokens show a red label. Most new tokens lose value fast, so only risk money you can afford to lose.

Wallets are ranked from trades this page has seen since you opened it. Profit is estimated at the bonding-curve price, before fees and slippage, and a short sample does not prove skill. Wallets that win by sniping may be bots or insiders. Following a wallet means buying after it, and it may sell first.

Scores describe observed early trading activity, not future returns. Unknown data is never treated as safe. Scans only while this page is open. Early Radar is not a buy signal.
